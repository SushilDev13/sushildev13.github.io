01Axion Ray × Cummins·Lead Analytics·2022 to 2026

A $60k pilot became a $3M account.

I led analytics on Axion Ray’s Cummins account: turning engine-failure data spread across warranty claims, repairs and technician notes into answers engineering could act on, fast enough that the customer kept asking for more.

  1. Problem

    Engineers were building pivot tables by hand from thousands of rows, while IoT logs, technician notes and service requests sat on servers. Off-the-shelf BI had hit its limits on data this inconsistent.

  2. Insight

    The losses weren’t hidden. They were fragmented.

    A fault only becomes a pattern once it’s linked across claims, repairs and escalations. The fix was a better join, not a better dashboard.

  3. What I did

    Built the workflows that link failures across sources, automated failure-code extraction with LLM pipelines (about 90% less manual ETL), and ran a 24-person delivery team at 95%+ SLA.

  4. Outcome

    $15M+ in potential quality losses put in front of engineering, and an account that grew 50× from its pilot.

02Publyq·Founder·Live, updated daily

10,902 complaints. All placed, all owned.

Chennai’s Corporation answers public complaints on X, but most records arrive without a ward. With no location there’s no pattern, and no one to hold accountable. Publyq fixes the location first; everything else follows.

Illustrative record. The structure and the pipeline are real.

  1. 01 / Collect

    It starts as a post.

    Residents raise issues on X. The Corporation replies with a complaint ID. Publyq collects those IDs every day.

  2. 02 / Read

    Then a record with no address.

    Each ID opens the Corporation’s own grievance record: department, complaint type, free-text location. About 58% arrive without a ward, so they can’t be counted against anyone.

  3. 03 / Place

    Then a place.

    Area, locality and street are matched against 3,484 known locations mapped to official ward boundaries. Every record leaves with a ward; zone and constituency follow.

  4. 04 / Own

    Finally, an owner.

    Each record gets one of 18 categories, safety, repeat and overdue flags, English and Tamil text, and the people responsible: the MLA, the zonal officer and the department.

The insight: accountability is geographic. A complaint becomes actionable the moment it has a place and an owner, so the hard problem was location resolution, not AI.
